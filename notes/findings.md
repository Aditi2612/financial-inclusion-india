# Key Findings

## 1. Accounts vs Credit — the inclusion gap
Rural India has the MOST bank accounts (1.16 billion) but the LEAST credit 
(₹23 lakh crore). Metropolitan has similar account numbers but ~7x the credit 
(₹161 lakh crore).

**Insight:** Account ownership (driven by Jan Dhan / DBT schemes) does not equal 
financial access. Accounts came to rural India; credit did not follow.
